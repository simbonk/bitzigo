Setup

  • Stand the phone or tablet up at waist height.
  • Move it back until everyone can be seen from the knees up.
  • Kids spread out in a row. Point an arm sideways to change character, both hands up when ready.
  • On iPhone or iPad, use Screen Mirroring to put it on the TV.

Full screen on iPhone

iPhone Safari can't make a web page full screen. To play with no browser bars, tap Share ⇪ then Add to Home Screen, and open Bitzigo from its new icon.
