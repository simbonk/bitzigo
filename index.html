How to set up

  • Prop the phone or laptop up at waist height, 3 to 4 metres back, so all kids fit in the picture from head to knees.
  • Kids stand wherever they like, spread out. Player 1 is whoever is furthest left on the TV. Each kid points an arm sideways to change character, then puts both hands up when ready.
  • On iPhone, use Screen Mirroring to show it on the TV.
  • Moves: jump over brick walls, punch (raise an arm) to smash crates on that side, lean your head away from crazy birds, both hands up to grab the rope over puddles. Floating hats are free prizes: just run into them.
  • Breaks: in throw time and snowball time, throw overhand to knock things down. In karate time, chop down hard to break boards. In fire time, point an arm out to aim the hose and spray the fires out. In bubble time, flap your arms to fly up into the sky and pop bubbles with your head.
  • 8 in a row makes you super charged: electric sparks and 1.5× points until you miss. Spin all the way round while super charged to cash it in for a mega bonus (keyboard: down on its own).
  • Keyboard test: P1 W/A/S/D, P2 T/F/G/H, P3 I/J/K/L, P4 arrow keys. Up = jump (or throw, chop or flap in the breaks), left/right = punch, left+right = hands up, down+left/right = lean.
  • Together puts everyone on the same course at the same time. Each kid still gets their own timing leeway, level and points. Own pace gives each kid their own course.
  • Short workouts (1 to 5 minutes) roll into the next round by themselves, up to 5 rounds, each in a new world with the same characters. Between rounds the scores show the running totals and a countdown; tap Stop to finish there. The heart check comes at the end.
  • Points are for timing: PERFECT, GREAT or GOOD, judged more gently for younger kids.
  • Difficulty is per kid: it starts gentle for their age, speeds up when they're nailing it and backs off after misses. It also ramps up a little as the workout timer runs down, and the last 15 seconds are double points.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: V camera view, ` debug info, C recalibrate, M mute, Enter start from the lobby, Esc end the workout.
